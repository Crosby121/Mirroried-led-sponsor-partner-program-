# Sponsor Package Framework

Mirroried LED uses package anchors for fast quoting, but every final deal is defined by its written deliverables.

## Package architecture

### Starter / Local Partner
Best for a business testing the program.
Potential components:
- one defined display placement;
- one approved digital/content mention;
- sponsor logo/name in approved campaign materials;
- campaign proof package;
- basic closeout report.

### Featured Partner
Best for a sponsor seeking repeated visibility across more than one controlled channel.
Potential components:
- multiple placements or larger-format placement;
- defined campaign duration;
- one or more approved video/content integrations;
- campaign asset rotation where supported;
- enhanced proof-of-performance;
- campaign recap.

### Premier / Presenting Partner
Best for a sponsor seeking a major branded activation.
Potential components:
- premium placement inventory;
- custom-branded infinity-mirror or LED activation;
- content/video integration;
- event or showroom activation;
- category-exclusivity option subject to approval;
- dedicated reporting and renewal review.

## Limited physical inventory
Examples of inventory that may be offered only when available:
- exterior-facing infinity-mirror advertising windows;
- interior sponsor mirrors;
- showroom display positions;
- event-specific display inventory.

Never sell more physical inventory than exists. Track reservation dates and availability in CRM/portal.

## Rate-card fields
Every sellable item should have:
- SKU / inventory ID
- Description
- Format/size
- Location/channel
- List price
- Minimum acceptable price
- Fulfillment cost
- Standard term
- Capacity/quantity
- Proof method
- Required creative specs
- Approval requirements

## Pricing rules
1. Quote the value of the full deliverable bundle, not an unsupported audience promise.
2. Discounts require a reason: longer term, prepaid commitment, bundled inventory, launch partner, or strategic value.
3. In-kind contributions require a documented fair-value estimate.
4. Hybrid deals must state both cash and in-kind components separately.
5. Exclusivity is an add-on with explicit scope, geography/channel, category, and term.

## Claims control
Do not attach monetary value to “impressions” unless measurement is credible and documented. Value physical placements, creative production, content deliverables, event presence, custom fabrication, and other controllable inventory instead.
