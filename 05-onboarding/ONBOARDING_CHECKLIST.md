# Sponsor Onboarding Checklist

## Before activation
- [ ] Signed agreement received
- [ ] Statement of Work finalized
- [ ] Cash/in-kind contribution schedule confirmed
- [ ] Sponsor primary contact confirmed
- [ ] Billing/contact information confirmed
- [ ] Campaign objective recorded
- [ ] Start/end dates recorded
- [ ] Deliverables entered into tracking system
- [ ] Inventory reserved
- [ ] Creative due dates recorded
- [ ] Approval path documented
- [ ] Category/exclusivity restrictions documented

## Sponsor asset intake
Collect only what is needed for the campaign:
- Legal business name
- Public brand name
- Approved logo files
- Brand colors/fonts where relevant
- Approved copy/tagline
- Website or landing-page URL
- QR destination if used
- Social handles if included
- Required legal/disclosure language
- Product images/video if included
- Named approver

## Creative QA
Before launch confirm:
- correct sponsor name/logo;
- correct dimensions/aspect ratio;
- readable copy;
- QR/link destination tested;
- required disclosure present;
- no unauthorized third-party IP;
- sponsor approval documented;
- production/display file archived.

## Internal launch handoff
Sales must hand operations:
- signed scope;
- exact deliverables;
- inventory IDs/locations;
- dates and frequencies;
- sponsor assets;
- proof requirements;
- exceptions or negotiated terms;
- sponsor contact and escalation contact.

## Launch confirmation
Record:
- activation date/time;
- person completing activation;
- placement/content IDs;
- initial proof photo/screenshot/link;
- any variance from scope;
- sponsor notification date.

## Rule
Do not activate from verbal instructions that conflict with the signed scope. Material changes require written approval and an updated scope/change record.
