# Mirroried LED Sponsor Program Playbook

## Positioning
Mirroried LED approaches sponsors as business partners. The company offers defined promotional inventory, content integrations, event/display opportunities, custom visual activations, and measurable campaign deliverables.

## Partnership types
- Cash sponsorship
- In-kind product sponsorship
- Service sponsorship
- Hybrid cash + in-kind
- Event/campaign sponsorship
- Product-placement/content partnership
- Showroom/display partnership
- Strategic local-business partnership

## Discovery-first sequence
1. Reach the correct department.
2. Explain Mirroried LED in one sentence.
3. Ask what the company is trying to achieve.
4. Learn how sponsorships are approved.
5. Identify budget/contribution range.
6. Match available inventory to the objective.
7. Send a written proposal.
8. Negotiate only written terms.
9. Contract and onboard.
10. Deliver, document, report, renew.

## Inventory categories
Inventory can include approved showroom mirror placements, exterior-facing display placements, interior sponsor mirrors, YouTube/video integrations, event integrations, social/content mentions, branded custom builds, product demonstrations, and campaign-specific placements.

Inventory is not sold as a vague promise of exposure. Every sellable unit must define location, format, quantity, duration, content/creative requirements, activation date, and proof method.

## Value exchange
For every deal record:
- Sponsor contribution
- Estimated fair value of contribution
- Mirroried LED deliverables
- Internal cost to fulfill
- Public/list price or negotiated value
- Campaign term
- Renewal option
- Exclusivity, if any

## Approval guardrails
Sales representatives may not independently promise:
- audience size or impressions not backed by measurement;
- guaranteed sales, leads, conversion, or ROI;
- perpetual/24-7 exposure unless technically and operationally guaranteed;
- category exclusivity without management approval;
- use of customer/private operational data;
- unapproved intellectual property, league/team marks, music, or third-party content.

## Recommended offer construction
Build proposals from components rather than forcing every sponsor into a rigid tier. Use packages as anchors, then customize against sponsor objectives.

## Campaign success
Success is measured primarily by fulfillment of contracted deliverables and secondarily by available campaign indicators such as content views, clicks, QR scans, inquiries, coupon use, event participation, or other documented metrics.
