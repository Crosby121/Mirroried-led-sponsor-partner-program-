Sponsor Partners contribute negotiated products, equipment, materials, services, cash, or a combination. In return, each approved agreement specifies a social-media promotional video, branding on our vehicles or mobile showrooms, negotiated screen advertising space, and a custom infinity mirror with the sponsor’s approved image. Mirror dimensions are agreed during negotiation. All benefits, dates and production details are confirmed in the signed agreement.

Business partnership—not a standard ad purchase.
