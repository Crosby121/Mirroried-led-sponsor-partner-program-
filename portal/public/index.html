MIRRORIED LED MARKETING PLATFORM

Similar purpose. Two different business environments.

Both programs create brand exposure through the Mirroried LED mobile showroom network, but the value exchange, customer journey and portal rules are different.

◇

Sponsor Partner Program

A business supplies approved products, equipment, materials or services that Mirroried LED needs for its truck, trailers, vans or showcase operation. Mirroried LED provides an agreed sponsor placement with a value equal to or less than the accepted contribution value.

▰

Advertising on the Go

A business purchases an advertising package, views approved event dates, chooses an event and one or both available showrooms, uploads its ad campaign and receives approved event-based display placement.

✓

Shared Operations

Both environments can share approved event records, creative assets, fulfillment evidence and reporting without exposing private fleet routes, device controls or internal operating data.

◎

Separate Accounting

Sponsor Partner value is tracked as contribution value versus sponsor placement value. Advertising is tracked as a paid package, event booking and campaign transaction.

SPONSOR PARTNER PROGRAM

Help equip the showcase. Receive agreed sponsor value in return.

This is a business partnership program—not a standard ad purchase. A partner may provide detailing supplies, tires, tools, fabrication equipment, power products, electronics, apparel, services or other approved items Mirroried LED actually needs.

Example: if Mirroried LED accepts $1,000 in needed products, the written partnership can provide up to $1,000 in agreed sponsor placement value inside the showroom or another approved sponsor location. The sponsor benefit may be equal to or less than the accepted contribution value.

1. ContributionApproved products, equipment, materials or services
2. ValuationDocument the accepted fair partnership value
3. Sponsor PlacementAssign agreed placement equal to or below that value
4. FulfillmentTrack the written placement and proof delivered
