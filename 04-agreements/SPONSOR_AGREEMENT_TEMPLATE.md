Sponsor will provide approved logos, artwork, copy, URLs, QR destinations, required disclosures, and usage instructions by the agreed deadline. Mirroried LED may reject creative that is unlawful, technically incompatible, misleading, unsafe, infringing, or inconsistent with program standards.

Each party retains ownership of its existing intellectual property. Sponsor grants Mirroried LED a limited license during the campaign term to use approved sponsor marks solely to perform the contracted deliverables. Sponsor represents that it has authority to provide those assets.

## 8. Proof and reporting
Mirroried LED will provide the proof-of-performance items listed in the Statement of Work. Reported metrics will be limited to data reasonably available and verifiable to Mirroried LED.
