# Sponsor CRM Pipeline

## Stages
1. **Target** — business identified
2. **Contacted** — outreach attempted
3. **Discovery** — needs/approval process being learned
4. **Qualified** — objective, decision path, contribution and timing fit
5. **Proposal** — written offer delivered
6. **Negotiation** — terms under discussion
7. **Contracted** — signed scope/terms
8. **Onboarding** — assets, approvals and inventory reservation
9. **Active** — campaign running
10. **Reporting** — fulfillment/reporting in progress
11. **Renewal** — next program discussion
12. **Closed/Lost/Nurture** — not currently active

## Required opportunity fields
- Company
- Contact
- Role
- Email/phone
- Lead source
- Business category
- Objective
- Partnership type
- Cash potential
- In-kind potential
- Decision-maker
- Approval criteria
- Restrictions
- Stage
- Probability
- Next action
- Next-action date
- Proposal amount/value
- Expected close date
- Notes

## Stage exit criteria
Do not move to Qualified until there is enough information to make a relevant proposal. Do not move to Contracted without written agreement. Do not move to Active until onboarding requirements are met.

## Renewal sequence
Begin renewal review before campaign expiration when practical. Review fulfillment first, then results, lessons, new objectives, available inventory, and next-term scope.

## Lost-deal reasons
Record one primary reason where known: no budget, timing, no fit, unreachable, competitor, legal/brand restriction, insufficient value, inventory unavailable, internal priority change, or other.

## Operating cadence
- Daily: next actions due
- Weekly: pipeline review and stalled deals
- Monthly: close rate, active sponsor count, booked value, fulfillment risks, renewals due
